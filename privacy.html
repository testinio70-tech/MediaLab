Privacy Policy for MediaLab

MediaLab is a private Telegram bot intended for authorized users only.

The bot processes only the Telegram user ID and the links submitted by the user in order to provide its media download functionality. MediaLab does not request or store passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal data.

Files are processed only to be delivered back to the user through Telegram. Access to the bot is restricted to users authorized by its administrator.

By using MediaLab, users confirm that they have the necessary rights or permission to download and process the requested content.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when new features or supported platforms are added.
